How we work

How fast can my store get its first delivery?

Most new retail partners receive their opening order within one week of onboarding. Once you're on a route, standing orders arrive on scheduled days with next-day fill-ins available across Los Angeles, Orange County, the Inland Empire and San Diego.

What brands and categories do you carry?

Our catalog spans the full Indian grocery basket: basmati and regional rice, dals and lentils, atta and specialty flours, spices and masala blends, pickles and chutneys, ready-to-eat meals, frozen breads and snacks, sweets, and beverages — from India's most recognized labels.

Do you support stores new to Indian groceries?

Yes. We help mainstream and specialty retailers build an Indian set from scratch — recommended assortments by store demographic, planograms, intro pricing and demo support, so the category earns its shelf space from week one.

What is your coverage area?

We deliver throughout Southern California — from Ventura and the San Fernando Valley down through Los Angeles, Orange County, the Inland Empire and San Diego — with our own trucks and drivers on every route.
